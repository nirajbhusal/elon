Musk spent the weekend in Washington. He sat at President Trump's own table at Thursday's state dinner for Xi Jinping, and his video of the evening became his most-liked post of the week. The rest of his feed was mostly rockets and trucks: Starship makes its first attempt to reach orbit on Monday evening Nepal time, and the Tesla Semi finally went into mass production. Running through it all was the story of OpenAI's "rogue" AI agents escaping their test environment. Musk calls it "troubling" and says it "keeps getting worse," and it is the thread that matters most for AI governance.
